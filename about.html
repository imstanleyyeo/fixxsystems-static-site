About

Our vision

To transform your business you need a partner, who continually brings you fresh ideas; a partner with deep industry knowledge and experience; and a partner who works shoulder to shoulder with you to help you realize and translate your plans to reality. FIXX Systems is this partner.

Established since 2000, we have the broadest capabilities with skill set that spans office systems furniture, hotels & resorts, residential, retail shop-in-shop, and high end luxury boutique.

Our team works with all levels, from Boards, Executive Management to Operations and Administration to understand and deliver business outcomes at speed, and with certainty, while helping you manage your downside risk. We are ambitious for our customers and want to achieve the quality and the timeliness that they have come to expect from FIXX Systems. We have the depth and the breadth of experiences to manage tough and complex assignments with extraordinary results. At FIXX Systems we have a solid resume and count among our many prestigious brand assignments, Longines, Mont Blanc, Omega, Coach, Emporio Armani, Gucci, Tag Heuer, Shiseido, Tissot, DFS Galleria at Myanmar Airport, Hanoi Airport, Ho Chi Minh Airport, Danang Airport, Siem Reap Airport and Corporate Offices of Certis CISCO, Lend Lease, RMIT, Hyatt Hotel Villas & Residences, and Spas.
